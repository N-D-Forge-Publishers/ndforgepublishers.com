About

An independent press, built to stay small.

Forge Publishers has spent two decades publishing fewer books, more carefully. We're editor-owned, we acquire directly, and we stay involved with a book long after its publication date.

Our Story

Why "Forge"

We named the press for the process, not the product. A forge doesn't shape metal in one strike — it heats, hammers, and tempers, again and again, until the material holds its new shape on its own. That's how we try to work with a manuscript: slow, deliberate passes, not a single edit and a release date.

We publish six to ten books a year across six genres. That number is a ceiling we chose on purpose — it's what lets every book on our list get a real editor, a real publicity plan, and a real chance.

What We Look For

Our standard

We're not chasing trends or a single house style. What we look for across every genre is the same: precise sentences, a clear sense of what the book is actually about, and a writer willing to revise. If that sounds like your manuscript, read our submission guidelines.
