For Authors

Submission guidelines

We read unsolicited manuscripts in all six genres, year-round.

What to send

A one-page query letter, a synopsis, and the first 30 pages (or 5 poems for poetry) as a single PDF.

Where to send it

Email everything to submissions@forgepublishers.example with your genre in the subject line.

Response time

We aim to respond to every query within 8–10 weeks. Simultaneous submissions are fine — just let us know.

Rights & Permissions

Translation, film, and reprint rights

For foreign rights, film and TV options, audio rights, or permission to reprint an excerpt, email rights@forgepublishers.example with the title, the rights you're requesting, and your intended use.
